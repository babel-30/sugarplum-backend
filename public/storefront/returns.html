Returns & Exchanges

We want you to feel good about your Brackish Roots purchase. Please contact us before sending an item back so we can help with the next steps.

Premade shirts and hats

Request a return within 14 days of delivery by emailing brackishroots@gmail.com with your order number and the items you wish to return. Once approved, mail your return within 7 days of approval. We will provide return instructions.

Items must be unworn, unwashed, unaltered, and free of odors, stains, pet hair, damage, or other signs of use. Include original tags if they were supplied.

Return shipping and refunds

Customers pay return shipping for size changes or a change of mind. Original shipping charges are nonrefundable for these returns. We do not charge a restocking fee or deduct previously free outbound shipping from an eligible refund.

After we receive and inspect an eligible return, we will issue the item-price refund to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. Your bank or payment provider may take additional time to post the refund.

Exchanges

We handle exchanges as a return and a new purchase. This lets you choose from currently available stock; replacement sizes, colors, and designs cannot be guaranteed.

Custom, personalized, and Build Your Own items

These items are made for your selections and are final sale. We do not accept returns or exchanges for a change of mind, customer-selected sizing, or customer-approved design and spelling choices. Please check your selections and personalization carefully before ordering.

Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, contact us promptly at brackishroots@gmail.com with your order number and photos of the item and any relevant packaging. This includes custom, personalized, and Build Your Own items. Brackish Roots will cover necessary return or replacement shipping for these issues.

Final-sale terms do not remove any rights you may have under applicable law.
