Inventory Management

Search for products, record your counted quantities, print fresh barcode labels, and send your +/- adjustments straight back to Square so everything stays in sync.
Tip: Use the scan-to-count box below with your Bluetooth barcode scanner. Each scan will add +1 to the Stock change for that SKU.

Stock change is an adjustment: + adds stock and − removes stock. Each scan adds +1. Selections and label quantities are kept while searching. To print labels, Select and set a number under Labels to print. Scan-to-count only affects the Stock change column.

For a physical stocktake, calculate counted stock minus current Square stock and enter that adjustment. Review the confirmation before applying it.

Job Supplies & Sourcing

Assign supplies manually to a custom project or paid custom/group order. Split the same product into stock and on-order lines when needed.

Stock pulls deduct the selected Square variations immediately when submitted. This records inventory use only; it does not charge the customer. Review project and order history to avoid allocating the same supplies twice. Square catalog checkout items use their normal inventory flow.

On order is a tracking record, not a supplier purchase. When supplies arrive, receive stock in Square first, then submit a stock pull if needed. Marking a line received here does not change Square inventory. Patch materials without a Square item can be described as on-order/manual notes; local patch availability flags stay manual.
